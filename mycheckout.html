CASE STUDY · 01

MY
CHECKOUT

A faster checkout experience for all.

Reducing friction and retail loss for self-checkout systems.

BRIEF

“The current self-checkout systems at retailers are accounted for 23% of their total unknown store losses. 43% of fixed self-checkout losses are believed to be caused by malicious behaviour.”

— ECR Retail Loss, 2022

How can a human-centred design approach to the SCO experience make shoppers feel loved?

SOLUTION

MyCheckout allows shoppers registered with a loyalty programme to pre-set default checkout settings at home. By using the API technology in the existing loyalty programme, shoppers can skip selecting checkout options when they tap their loyalty card to activate those default settings.

CO-BENEFITS

FOR SHOPPERS

  • Cuts checkout time by 10 seconds.
  • Shortens queues by improving checkout efficiency.
  • Minimises the risk of repeated-click errors.
  • Improves self-checkout accessibility.

FOR RETAILERS

  • Boosts sales capacity by reducing queue times.
  • Encourages loyalty-programme engagement, helping reduce malicious behaviour associated with feeling anonymous.
  • Improves checkout experience, reducing frustration-driven malicious behaviour and potential retail losses.

TOUCHPOINTS

Loyalty app interface design

The function is integrated into the existing supermarket app, where shoppers customise their carrier-bag, payment, receipt, and accessibility default settings before their next self-checkout.

New service activations

In-store touchpoints at the self-checkout zone promote the new service.

Self-checkout interface design

Saved preferences are applied automatically when the user scans their loyalty card.

RETAIL SPECIALIST FEEDBACK

“In 2025, changes to regulations on digital equipment are coming and they are wide-reaching. This is a positive way of doing that.”

PROCESS

Research

What do consumers like about self-checkout? Field research at retailers informed the opportunity.

Insights

Convenience, speed, privacy, and a feeling of control are central to a positive self-checkout experience.

Ideation

Testing

21 regular self-checkout users completed standard and MyCheckout routes using a clickable prototype.

100% of participants noticed the customised checkout route was faster than the standard route.

−10s average time saved with the clickable prototype.
