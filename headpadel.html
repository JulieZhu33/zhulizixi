CASE STUDY · 04

REPLAY

A racket trade-in service that turns players’ progression into an opportunity for HEAD Padel to build long-term customer loyalty.

BRIEF

HEAD Padel is an Austrian sports brand with expertise in racket sports. The goal is to attract new players to padel while building stronger brand engagement in the UK.

Market position

HEAD held 8% of the padel-racket market, ranking fourth in 2021, behind Babolat (16%), Bullpadel (13%), and Adidas (9%).

Strengths

Competitive pricing among mainstream padel brands
A comprehensive product ecosystem
A strong reputation in racket sports

SOLUTION

REPLAY aims to promote HEAD Padel as the first choice for players ready to level up.

TOUCHPOINTS

The solution applies the EAST behavioural framework — easy, attractive, social, and timely — to define the customer-experience design parameters.

TARGETING AUDIENCE

Young progressing players

Age:
22-29
Playing level:
Beginner→intermediate
Budget:
Medium
Behaviour:
Increasing frequency of play, improving lifestyle as they become financially independent
Current equipment:
Entry-level, inexpensive, second-hand or previously rented racket
Psychological Motivation:
To make their growing commitment to padel tangible

RESEARCH METHODS

Targeting audience

age 18-29

Experience

Existing padel players

Region

United Kingdom

Quantitative

N= 23 Respondents Online questionnaire (5 Mins)

Qualitative

N = 6 Respondents Semi-structured interview (20 mins)

Quantitative research objectives

  • How they discovered padel
  • How long they've played
  • How frequently they play
  • Why they picked up padel
  • Racket ownership
  • How much they'd spend
  • Which brands they know

Qualitative research objectives

  • Barriers to play more
  • What motivate them to continue to play
  • When did they start to purchase equipments
  • What motivate them to choose a specific brand
